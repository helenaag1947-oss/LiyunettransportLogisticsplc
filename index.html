01 / COMPANY PROFILE

A dependable partner for freight movement.

Liyunet Transport and Logistics P.L.C. is located in Addis Ababa, near Kality Main Custom Office. The company profile states more than 10 years of experience in transport and logistics services in Ethiopia.

The company is focused on effective and efficient services, with an emphasis on innovation, quality, safety, security and flexibility. Its management and staff bring experience from transport, logistics, business management and related fields.
